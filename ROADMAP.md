# Roadmap

The roadmap is tracked with [slicer](.slicer/). The rendered view is
[.slicer/render/ROADMAP.md](.slicer/render/ROADMAP.md) (also `ROADMAP.html`).

- `slicer status` — next item, progress, and blockers
- `slicer list` / `slicer show S01` — items and slice specifications
- `slicer tui` — browse and reorder interactively

Do not hand-edit `.slicer/` files; change tracking state through the `slicer` CLI.
